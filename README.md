# Moon

- Lunar terrain and imagery: [NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14959/) and [LRO / LROC / LOLA Moon maps](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/). NASA supplies the original topography and color imagery; the displayed terrain retains its geometry with derived smooth normals.
- [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three), including its controls, loader, and utilities: MIT license, preserved in [THREE-LICENSE.txt](THREE-LICENSE.txt).
- Star-field approach adapted from [Drei Stars](https://github.com/pmndrs/drei/blob/master/src/core/Stars.tsx): MIT license; the copyright and permission notice are preserved in [space-background.js](space-background.js).
